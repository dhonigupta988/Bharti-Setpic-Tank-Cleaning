About BST

Built on 8+ years of getting the dirtiest job done right

Bharti Septic Tank Clearance started with a single tanker and a simple promise: show up fast, clean properly, and never cut corners on safety. Today BST runs multiple super-suction units across Ranchi, registered with the local municipal body and trusted by hundreds of households, hospitals, hotels and apartments.

Every crew member is trained for safe, hygienic handling of septic and sewage waste — no manual scavenging, no shortcuts. Waste is disposed of responsibly, in line with Swachh Bharat safety standards, not dumped on the roadside.
